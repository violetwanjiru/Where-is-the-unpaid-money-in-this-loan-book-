Is loan delinquency concentrated or widespread, and where should collections focus first?

Concentrated. 60 of 1,868 loans hold almost all the unpaid money. The rest of the book repays.

1,868 digital loans, 840 customers, funded 30 March to 30 May 2026. Amounts in shillings. Method, findings and recommendations are in the repository README.

The decision this supports Credit and Operations should work the 60 loans with Late status and leave lending to everyone else unchanged: those 60 loans, 3.2% of the book, hold almost all of the 1.57 million shillings unpaid, and the ten largest borrowers have repaid every loan they took. PAR30+ here is a proxy built on original principal and recorded days late, so read it as the size of the problem, not a precise balance. Operations should confirm outstanding balances on the 60 loans, and Credit should name the per-customer limit so a capped pilot for proven borrowers can be approved, before the next portfolio review.
